Request early access How it works

AI agents are spreading fast. The people fixing their mistakes are often domain experts who can’t change them. So they accept the output, rework it in Excel, Word, or email, and move on. The fix lives in a file, and rarely anywhere the agent’s owners can see it.

Farfield starts where the agent stack ends.

Other observability tools stop when the agent run completes. Farfield follows the output into the tools and processes employees work in, to capture what gets fixed before the work is truly done.

  1. Compare

    Raw AI output against the final, human-approved work in Excel, Word, email, and internal systems.

  2. Detect

    Recurring correction patterns across employees, not one-off edits.

  3. Quantify

    The time and dollar cost of each pattern.

  4. Surface

    The highest-value fixes, back to engineering and operations.

An accountability layer for deployed agents.

Structured feedback from real employee behavior, so internal AI systems can improve over time.

Built for the people who fix what AI gets wrong.

Every day, analysts and operators catch what agents miss. That judgment is valuable, and today it disappears into the file they send. Farfield makes it count.

  1. Fix it once

    When the same correction shows up across a team, it goes to the people who can change the agent, so the whole team stops repeating it.

  2. Your judgment becomes signal

    Corrections are treated as expert input to the system. Farfield measures the agent, not the person.

  3. No prompts required

    Our goal is for domain experts to improve enterprise AI without needing to understand prompts, models, or agent infrastructure.
